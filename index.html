Carepaz Payments

Payment reconciliation automation by Carepaz Industries Sdn. Bhd.

Carepaz Payments is an internal tool operated by Carepaz Industries Sdn. Bhd. (Malaysia). It helps reconcile driver payment vouchers against bank transfer records and organizes the results in the company's own Google Drive workspace, where a reviewer approves each match before month-end processing.

This is a private business tool; it is not offered as a public service.
