Privacy Policy

Carepaz Payments — last updated 16 October 2026

Carepaz Payments is an internal automation tool operated by Carepaz Industries Sdn. Bhd. ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the tool handles information when it connects to Google services.

What the tool accesses

With the user's authorization, the tool uses Google Drive access to create and read files and folders inside the user's own Google Drive (for example, storing payment voucher images and monthly ledger spreadsheets). It does not access any other Google account data, and it does not access Drive files that were not created for this purpose.

How information is handled

Files processed by the tool remain in the user's own Google Drive. Copies may be processed on company-controlled computers for matching and review. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data retention

Records are kept in the company's Google Drive only as long as needed for accounting and audit purposes, after which they may be deleted.
